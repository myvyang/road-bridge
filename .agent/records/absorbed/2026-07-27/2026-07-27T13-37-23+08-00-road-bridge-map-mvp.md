## Engineering Context

Important transferred judgment from `stock_analysis`: road and bridge companies are toll-right asset portfolios. Company-level profit is not enough; research must split down to individual roads and bridges, with toll period, remaining years, traffic, freight share, revenue, profit, capex, construction/diversion, expansion and renewal issues.

The first version deliberately marks embedded values as `sample` or `needs_source`. Real data must later come from annual reports, prospectuses, exchange disclosures, toll-right approvals, government documents, and official operating disclosures. If annual reports disclose several roads in aggregate, the model should preserve that disclosure scope instead of forcing artificial allocation.
